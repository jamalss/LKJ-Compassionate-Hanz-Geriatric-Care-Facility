Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This policy explains what information this website handles when you visit it, and what happens when you contact LKJ Compassionate Hanz Geriatric Care Facility through it.

Who we are

LKJ Compassionate Hanz Geriatric Care Facility, 1540 Southern Main Road, Cochran Village, Guapo, Point Fortin, Trinidad & Tobago. WhatsApp: +1 (868) 295-5909 or +1 (868) 396-1806.

What this website does not do

  • It does not use cookies.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • It does not have user accounts and does not store anything you type.

The contact form and WhatsApp buttons

The contact form does not send your details to a server. When you press “Send via WhatsApp”, your browser opens WhatsApp with your message already written. Nothing is sent until you press send inside WhatsApp yourself.

Once you send a WhatsApp message, we receive your name, phone number and whatever you choose to write. We use this only to reply to your enquiry and to discuss care for your loved one. We do not sell or share it for marketing.

WhatsApp is run by WhatsApp LLC (part of Meta). Your use of WhatsApp is covered by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

Please do not send medical details through the website form. We will discuss care needs with you directly.

The care-needs guide

The “How Can We Support Your Family?” tool works only inside your browser. Your selections are not saved or sent anywhere unless you choose to continue to WhatsApp.

Services this website relies on

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on this page. Your browser requests the fonts from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps opens only if you press “Get Directions”.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold from your WhatsApp conversations with us, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Message us on WhatsApp and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If this website starts using new tools (for example analytics or online booking), we will update this page and the date at the top.
