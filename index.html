About Us

A Place of Care, Comfort and Belonging

At LKJ Compassionate Hanz, every resident is more than a resident—they are family. Our approach combines professional geriatric support with compassion, dignity, faith, and meaningful human connection.

Our team works closely with each family to understand individual needs, so care always feels personal rather than routine.

We care for every resident as Christ calls us to care for one another with compassion, dignity, and love.

What We Offer

Services Built Around Every Resident

Personalised Daily Care

Everyday assistance shaped around each resident's routines, preferences, and comfort.

Medication Support

Assistance with medication routines, coordinated with each family's guidance.

Nutritious Meals

Balanced, home-style meals prepared with care for individual dietary needs.

Mobility Assistance
