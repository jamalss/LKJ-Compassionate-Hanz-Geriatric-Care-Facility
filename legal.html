2. The law we follow

We are based in Trinidad and Tobago. We handle personal information in line with the General Privacy Principles in section 6 of the Data Protection Act, Chap. 22:04. In plain terms, we:

  • take responsibility for personal information in our care;
  • tell you why we collect information before or when we collect it;
  • collect, use or share information only with your knowledge and consent, or where the law allows;
  • collect only what we need, and keep it only as long as we need it;
  • keep information accurate and up to date;
  • protect information with safeguards suited to how sensitive it is, with extra care for health information;
  • make our practices public (this page);
  • let you see the information we hold about you, and challenge how we handle it; and
  • only allow information to go abroad where it is protected to a comparable standard.

Some parts of the Data Protection Act have not yet been brought into force. We apply these principles in full anyway, as our standard. We serve families in Trinidad and Tobago. If you visit from another country, we apply the same standard to your information.

4. Health and other sensitive information

Under the Data Protection Act, information about physical or mental health is sensitive personal information, and deserves extra care. So:

  • Please do not send medical details in your first message or through the website form. A general description of the support you are looking for is enough to start.
  • Where health information is genuinely needed, for example to understand whether we can meet someone’s care needs, we will ask for it directly, explain why, and collect only what is needed.
  • If you share information about another person, such as a parent or relative, please do so only if they have agreed, or if you are entitled to act for them (for example, as their next of kin or legal representative).
  • The care-needs guide on this website is a conversation starter only. It is not a medical assessment and stores nothing.

5. How we use your information

We use information you send us only to:

  • reply to your enquiry and answer your questions;
  • arrange visits to the facility;
  • understand your family’s situation and discuss whether and how we can help;
  • keep a record of our conversation while your enquiry is active; and
  • meet any legal obligation that applies to us.

We will not add you to marketing lists, send you promotional messages you did not ask for, or use your information to make automated decisions about you. If we ever want to send updates, we will ask first, and you can say no or stop at any time.

6. How WhatsApp fits in

Every button on this website that contacts us opens WhatsApp. When you tap one, you leave this website and enter WhatsApp, a service run by WhatsApp LLC, part of Meta Platforms, Inc.

  • Nothing is sent until you press send inside WhatsApp. You can edit or delete the prepared message first.
  • WhatsApp says personal messages are protected by end-to-end encryption. WhatsApp itself still processes some information about your account and use of its service, under WhatsApp’s Privacy Policy. We do not control that.
  • WhatsApp and LKJ Compassionate Hanz are each responsible for their own handling of your information.
  • Within LKJ Compassionate Hanz, your messages are seen only by the people who manage our two business WhatsApp numbers, and only for the purposes in section 5.

If you would rather not use WhatsApp, you are welcome to visit us at the address in section 1.

7. Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies and no browser storage of any kind: no local storage, no session storage and no tracking identifiers. It also uses no analytics tools (such as Google Analytics), no advertising or social-media pixels (such as the Meta Pixel) and no cross-site tracking.

Because nothing is stored on your device, there is no cookie banner to accept or decline. Your browser may keep its own ordinary cache of page files to load them faster. You can clear that in your browser settings at any time.

10. Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell or share your personal information. Ever.

LKJ Compassionate Hanz does not sell, rent or trade personal information. We do not share it with anyone for advertising, marketing or profiling. This website contains no advertising or tracking technology that could do so.

Some privacy laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), give people the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of their personal information. Because we do neither, there is nothing you need to opt out of. If you would like this confirmed in writing, message us on WhatsApp (section 19).

13. Security

On this website:

  • every page is served over encrypted HTTPS;
  • a strict Content Security Policy blocks scripts from any source other than this website;
  • there is no database, login system or form storage that could be breached; and
  • external links open safely, without passing information about this website to the sites they open.

For WhatsApp messages:

  • our business phones are protected with a passcode or biometric lock;
  • WhatsApp two-step verification is switched on for our business numbers; and
  • we do not forward enquiry messages to people who do not need them.

No system is completely secure. If we learn of a security incident that puts your information at risk, we will tell you without undue delay and explain what we are doing about it.

14. Your rights and how to use them

At any time, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and how we use it;
  • give you a copy of that information;
  • correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information, unless we must keep it by law or under an admission agreement;
  • stop contacting you, or withdraw any consent you have given; and
  • explain or reconsider any concern you raise about how we handled your information.

How: send us a WhatsApp message saying what you would like us to do. We may ask a simple question to confirm it is really you, so that we never give your information to someone else. We aim to respond within 30 days. Making a request is free, and it will never affect how we treat you or your family.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may seek independent legal advice or raise the matter with the relevant authority in Trinidad and Tobago.

15. Children

This website is meant for adults arranging care for older relatives. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has contacted us, message us and we will delete the information.

16. Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboards or magnification. It is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. That includes text alternatives for meaningful images, sufficient colour contrast, full keyboard access, a clear heading structure, and never relying on colour alone.

If anything on this website is difficult for you to use, please tell us on WhatsApp. We will fix it, or give you the information another way.
